### Lamellar Horse Armor
**Category:** Mount Armor
**Base Price:** 3 gold coins
**Bulk:** Very heavy and large
**Description:** 
	Can be equipped on any horse. This horse's physical armor increases by 5.
	When equipped, the horse's base movement speed is reduced by 1 meter. 

### Warded Horse Armor
**Category:** Mount Armor
**Base Price:** 3 gold coins
**Bulk:** Very heavy and large
**Description:** 
	Can be equipped on any horse. This horse's magical armor increases by 5.
	When equipped, the horse's base movement speed is reduced by 1 meter.
