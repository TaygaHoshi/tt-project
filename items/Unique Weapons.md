## One Handed Weapons
### Pactblade
**Category:** Cavalry sword
**Base Price:** 3 gold coins
**Bulk:** One handed heavy weapon
**Description:** 
	Whenever you perform a damaging weapon attack with this sword, you can sacrifice health equal to your potency as part of it. 
	Potency bonus to that attack's damage is doubled. 
	This weapon is very rare and can only be purchased from speciality stores.

## Off-hand Weapons
### Parrying Shield
**Category:** Shield
**Base Price:** 100 iron coins
**Bulk:** Off-hand weapon
**Description:**
	A smaller and lighter shield which is as good at parrying as regular shields.
	While it does not grant any physical armor, it does not incur the weapon precision penalty that a heavier shield would. 

## Two handed weapons
### Repeating Crossbow
**Category:** Crossbow
**Base Price:** 1 gold coin
**Bulk:** Two handed weapon
**Description:** 
	A crossbow with a 5 bolt magazine.
	This crossbow can be fired up to 5 times without reloading. Then, it must be reloaded as a full turn action. 
	This weapon is very rare and can only be purchased from speciality stores.
